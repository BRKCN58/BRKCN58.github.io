Money City

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

This policy explains how the Android game Money City ("the game"), published by LapaGames ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your e-mail address, phone number or contacts. Your city, progress and settings are saved on your device. If you uninstall the game, this data is deleted.

Leaderboards

When your device is online, the game signs in to Google Firebase anonymously (a random ID, no e-mail or personal account) and sends the following to Google Cloud Firestore so the global leaderboards can be shown: the player name you choose in the game, your city name, your level, your coins and your total rent earned. This information is visible to other players on the leaderboards. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your player name. To have your leaderboard entry deleted, e-mail us your in-game player name at brkcnimdt@gmail.com.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice later in the game settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example which levels players reach and which features they use) and to improve it. It collects an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate location (country) and in-game events. We do not use it to identify you. See the Firebase privacy information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when rent is ready). You can turn them off in the game settings or in Android settings at any time.

In-app purchases

If the game offers purchases, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your leaderboard entry at any time (see Leaderboards). Uninstalling the game deletes the data stored on your device. For data processed by Google for ads and analytics, see your Google account and Android settings. For any question, contact us at brkcnimdt@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
