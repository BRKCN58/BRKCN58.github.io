Money City: Builder Tycoon

Money City is a relaxing city builder and tycoon game where you become the mayor and the landlord. Build homes, sign rent contracts with your tenants, collect rent and turn a small green village into a rich megalopolis. You can play it offline, in short sessions or for hours.

Features

BUILD YOUR DREAM CITY

Place cozy cottages, apartments, glass towers and skyscrapers. Open cafés, pizza shops, cinemas, banks and malls. Connect everything with asphalt roads, paving stones or cobblestone streets, and decorate with parks, fountains, flower beds and statues.

COLLECT RENT, SIGN CONTRACTS

Every home needs a tenant. Choose a rent contract from 1 minute to 3 days: the longer the contract, the bigger the payout. Quick contracts while you play, long ones before you sleep. Rent notifications tell you when your money is ready.

EXPLORE A HUGE ISLAND

Buy new land and expand across five regions: green meadows, forest, ice fields, desert and swamp. Each region has its own special homes like log cabins, igloos, adobe houses and stilt houses.

10 WORLD WONDERS

Build famous landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, the Colosseum, the Great Pyramid, the Taj Mahal and the Statue of Liberty. Each wonder boosts the income of the buildings around it.

A CITY THAT FEELS ALIVE

Watch day turn into night, rain and wind roll in and the seasons change. Citizens walk the streets, cars drive, boats sail and tourists visit your wonders.

HAPPY CITIZENS PAY MORE

Keep your people happy with parks, shops and transport like bus stops, metro stations, a port and an airport. Fulfill citizen requests to raise happiness and your rent.

UPGRADE AND GROW

Upgrade buildings up to 10 stars for more income. Complete quests, unlock achievements, earn new city titles from Village to Megalopolis and join weekly events for exclusive decorations.

CENTRAL BANK

Short on cash? Take a bank loan. Got extra? Put it into savings and earn daily interest, even while you are offline.

TAXES, PROTESTS AND RIOTS

You are the mayor, so you set the tax rate. Taxes fill your treasury, but push them too high and citizens protest. Ignore them and a riot breaks out and shops get looted.

PUBLIC SERVICES

Build schools, police and fire stations, hospitals and a university to boost rent and happiness in the neighborhood.

CITY TASKS AND MINI-GAMES

Rescue a cat from a tree, fix power lines and pipes, put out fires, patch potholes and crack a safe. 13 different mini-games with rewards.

NEW REWARDS EVERY DAY

A 7-day login streak, daily tasks and a chest, a lucky wheel, a collection album, hidden treasure on new land and a tour bus full of tourists.

LEADERBOARD

Compete with mayors around the world in level, wealth and rent income. The number one city gets a golden city hall.

Frequently asked questions

What is Money City?

Money City is a free city builder and tycoon game for Android. You build homes and businesses, sign rent contracts with tenants, collect rent and grow a small village into a megalopolis on a large island with five regions.

Can I play Money City offline?

Yes. Money City works without an internet connection. You only need internet to watch optional ads for bonus gold bars. The leaderboard also needs internet.

How does rent work in Money City?

Each home needs a rent contract. Contracts last from 1 minute to 3 days, and longer contracts pay much more. When rent is ready you collect it with a tap, or use gold bars to collect it right away.

Is Money City pay to win?

No. Everything in the game can be earned by playing. Gold bars come from rewarded ads, the daily gift and achievements.

What languages does Money City support?

English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Azerbaijani, Hindi and Chinese. The game starts in your phone's language automatically.

Does Money City have a bank?

Yes. At the Central Bank you can take a loan or put money into savings that earn interest every day, even while the game is closed.

Does Money City have a leaderboard?

Yes. There are three world rankings: level, wealth and rent income. You choose your own player name, and the number one city gets a golden city hall.

Is Money City like other classic city builder and tycoon games?

If you enjoyed the classic social city builders where you collected rent from your buildings, Money City brings that feeling to mobile: build, collect, expand and decorate, at your own pace and offline.

Money City is free to play. Ads are optional: watch one only when you want a bonus, like extra gold bars.

If you love city building, tycoon and management games, start building your Money City today.
